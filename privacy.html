Privacy Policy

This policy describes Affiliate Intelligence Studio (AIS).

Information processed

The analytics application may process manually entered video metrics, product information, screenshots and account identifiers. If TikTok integration is enabled, AIS will request authorization before accessing permitted account and video information.

Purpose and processing

Information is used for analytics, content planning and strategy evaluation. The application uses Google Apps Script and Google Sheets for operation and storage. When AI analysis or OCR is explicitly requested, relevant input may be processed by configured AI providers, including Google Gemini and Meta.

Access and retention

The dashboard is intended for authorized access. Records remain in the configured storage until removed by its operator. This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information under its own privacy policy.

Control and requests

Users may stop using AIS, request deletion of their records from the operator, and revoke any future TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings. Contact the AIS operator through the project owner profile.
